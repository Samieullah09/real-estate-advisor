\# Use Cases



\## 1. Who can use this app?

| User | How it helps |

|---|---|

| \*\*First-time home buyer\*\* | Checks if a property is a good investment before paying. Sees an estimated price after 5 years. |

| \*\*Property investor\*\* | Compares many properties quickly. Finds cities and property types with better growth chances. |

| \*\*Real estate agent or broker\*\* | Shows clients a data-based view instead of only opinions. |

| \*\*Builder or developer\*\* | Sees which factors (transport, infrastructure, amenities) improve investment appeal. |

| \*\*Student or data analyst\*\* | Learns a full project: data cleaning, EDA, two models, MLflow and deployment. |



\## 2. Example situations



\*\*Situation 1: Is this flat worth buying?\*\*

A buyer finds a 3 BHK apartment in Bangalore for Rs 185 lakh. They open the \*Investment Advisor\* page and enter the details.

The app shows a verdict (Good Investment or Needs Further Review), a confidence level, the expected price after 5 years and how the price compares with the city median.



\*\*Situation 2: Which area should I look at?\*\*

An investor opens the \*EDA Explorer\* page and filters by state, BHK and price range. The charts show where price per sq ft is higher and how transport and availability change the Good Investment share.



\*\*Situation 3: Which model can I trust?\*\*

A reviewer opens the \*Model Performance\* page. They see accuracy, F1, ROC-AUC, the confusion matrix and the feature importance, so they can judge how reliable the predictions are.



\## 3. How to use the app

1\. Open https://estateiq-advisor.streamlit.app/

2\. Choose \*\*Investment Advisor\*\* in the sidebar.

3\. Fill in the property details and press \*\*Get Investment Analysis\*\*.

4\. Read the verdict, the 5-year price and the comparison charts.

5\. Use \*\*EDA Explorer\*\* to understand the market and \*\*Model Performance\*\* to see how good the models are.



\## 4. Business value

\- Saves time: one page replaces many manual checks.

\- Gives a clear, data-based starting point for a big money decision.

\- Easy to extend: new cities or better data can be added without changing the app design.



\## 5. Important note

The app is for learning and demonstration. The dataset is synthetic, and the Good Investment label comes from a documented growth model, not from real market history.

It is \*\*not financial advice\*\*. Always check real prices, legal papers and talk to a professional before buying a property.
